1n Rationale — every decision maps to honesty
Make honest evidence legible and credible.
01One truth language, learned once. source_kind = shape + hue, confidence = a neutral 3-bar meter, redaction = a treatment on the value itself, provenance = the same shapes reused. A viewer who reads the legend once can read every surface — graph, packet, compare, inspector.
02Unproven is always dashed. future / not-yet-collected renders with dashed rings, dashed borders, hollow meters — an encoding that survives grayscale and colorblindness. A simulated or future claim can never be styled into looking collectable; the geometry itself refuses.
03Confidence is a separate channel from origin. The meter is neutral ink, never colored by source hue — so high-confidence-derived and low-confidence-collectable stay distinguishable, and no color can "wash" a weak claim upward.
04Withheld ≠ missing. Redacted values keep their place in the layout and wear a lock chip with the detector name; blocked values are a solid chip. The reader sees that something exists and was deliberately not shown — the opposite of hiding.
05Red is rationed. The failure hue appears in exactly two places: recorded failures and unsupported-claims chips. "Not observed" states are slate-dashed, calm, confident — absence of evidence is a finding, not an alarm.
06Density by grouping, not amputation. The 8-node cap and "+62 more" chip are gone. Nodes group by run with expandable invocation/artifact clusters and level-of-detail on zoom — all 70 nodes stay reachable, and the count readout ("70 / 70 shown") proves nothing is hidden.
07The command bar tells the truth about itself. Reframed from chat to a ⌘K palette: the full vocabulary is listed and grouped, every result says what it does, and the footer states its status — a local filter over loaded data, never persisted, never evidence.
08A readable floor. Type never drops below 11px; weights snap to 400/500/600/700; one frosted-glass recipe at three elevations; one 4px spacing scale. The instrument feels engineered because it is measured.
Handoff Token names for the engineering port: --truth-collectable/-derived/-simulated/-future (+ -tint variants per theme), --n0…--n9 neutral ramp, --space-1…7 (4-48), --radius-control/chip/card/panel, --elev-1/2/3, --text-display/title/heading/body/caption/mono. Dark mode = same tokens re-mapped under [data-theme="dark"]. Every mock on this canvas uses only these values.
